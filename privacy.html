Bead Melt

Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Bead Melt (“the game”) is made by SilentDev. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Bead Melt on Android or iPhone.

In short

Bead Melt does not collect, sell or share any personal information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and works offline.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send data to us.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, daily streak) and the sound setting are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

If you have device backup turned on (Android backup or iCloud), your phone may include this progress in its backup. That backup is run by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; we cannot see it.

Sharing a keychain

When you tap Share, the game draws an image of your keychain on your device and opens your phone’s share sheet. The image and text go only to the app and people you choose. We never receive them. The image is kept in the game’s private cache on your device.

Third-party services

This version of Bead Melt contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. If a future version adds ads (for example Google AdMob) or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the app’s store privacy details before that version is released.

Children

Bead Melt is a general audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete all game data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
