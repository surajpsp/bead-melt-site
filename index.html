Bead Melt

Sort beads. Make pixel art. Melt it into a keychain.

Bead Melt is a relaxing color sort puzzle. Every tube you fill with one color pours onto a hidden pegboard, until the pixel art appears and the iron melts it into a keychain for your album.

Get it on Google Play Plays offline. No account, no timers.

How the bead sort puzzle works

  1. Sort by color

    Tap a tube to lift its top beads, tap another to drop them on the same color or into an empty tube.

  2. Pour into the picture

    A tube full of one color pours its beads onto the pegboard, and the pixel art grows bead by bead.

  3. Iron your keychain

    Finish every color and the iron melts the picture into a keychain that stays in your album.

Questions

Is Bead Melt free?
Yes, Bead Melt is free to download and play on Android.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. The game plays fully offline and saves your progress on your phone.
Is there a time limit or a way to lose?
No. There are no timers and no fail screen, so you can play at your own pace.
How is it different from ball sort or water sort?
The sorting is the same calm idea, but every finished tube builds a pixel art picture that you keep as a fuse bead keychain.
When do I get a new daily picture?
At midnight in your own time zone.
